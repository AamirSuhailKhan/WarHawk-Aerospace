TERMS OF SERVICE

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 17, 2026 · APPLICABLE JURISDICTION: INDIA

1. Nature of Business

Warhawk Aerospace provides enterprise Drone-as-a-Service (DaaS), AI-assisted perimeter surveillance, and industrial aerial inspection solutions for commercial, industrial, infrastructure, and institutional clients in India. Warhawk Aerospace is a commercial aerial managed-services and software provider, not a munitions or weapons manufacturer.

2. Operational & Aviation Compliance (DGCA)

All commercial aerial operations conducted by Warhawk Aerospace strictly comply with the Drone Rules 2021, Drone (Amendment) Rules 2022/2023, and guidelines issued by the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) and the Ministry of Civil Aviation (MoCA):

  • Commercial flights are conducted using DGCA Type-Certified commercial airframes with valid Unique Identification Numbers (UIN).
  • Piloting is executed or supervised exclusively by DGCA-certified Remote Pilot Certificate (RPC) holders.
  • Flight missions are planned strictly within permitted Digital Sky airspace classifications (Green, Yellow with prior ATC/airport authorization, or Red with explicit central government permissions).
  • Operations are restricted to Visual Line of Sight (VLOS) unless explicit, authorized experimental/operational BVLOS permissions have been granted for the specific project corridor.

3. Site Feasibility & Service Quotations

Any pricing estimates, package tiers, or operational timelines displayed on this website are indicative commercial hypotheses. Binding commercial proposals are provided only following a comprehensive operational site feasibility assessment covering facility acreage, terrain, airspace zoning, radiofrequency interference, flight frequency, and required sensor payloads.

4. Client Site Authorization & Private Property

Clients contracting Warhawk DaaS operations must provide verified legal authorization and access permissions for the designated flight zone. Client private property ownership does not supersede statutory DGCA airspace restrictions or national security flight zones.

5. AI Decision Boundaries & Human Supervision

Our proprietary computer vision and telemetry analytics identify and flag observable physical anomalies (such as perimeter breaches, thermal gradients, equipment wear, and vehicle movements). All tactical or operational response decisions remain strictly under human client supervision and management.

6. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These terms and any resulting commercial service agreements shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. Courts of competent jurisdiction in Bengaluru / New Delhi shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
