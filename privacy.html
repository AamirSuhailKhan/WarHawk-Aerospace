PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 17, 2026 · COMPLIANCE: DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION ACT (INDIA, 2023)
Core Principle: Warhawk Aerospace strictly segregates Website Customer Inquiry Data from Enterprise Aerial Drone Surveillance & Inspection Data. We process customer personal data transparently, adhere to data minimization principles, and implement rigorous technical safeguards.

1. Purpose and Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how Warhawk Aerospace ("Warhawk", "we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, protects, and disposes of personal data in connection with:

  • Visitors interacting with our digital web platform (https://warhawk.aero) and submitting B2B service inquiries;
  • Authorized enterprise clients contracting our Drone-as-a-Service (DaaS) and AI-powered industrial inspection operations across India.

2. Website Inquiry Data Collection & Lawful Basis

When you submit an enterprise inquiry or request a site feasibility proposal through our website, we collect the following limited information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full name, work email address, telephone number;
  • Enterprise Profile: Company/organization name, industry sector, approximate site acreage;
  • Operational Scope: Requested service package and project requirements message;
  • Technical Metadata: Timestamp, consent confirmation record, client IP (for rate-limiting and anti-abuse).

Lawful Basis: Data is processed on the basis of your explicit affirmative consent and for taking steps at your request prior to entering into a commercial enterprise service contract.

3. Data Retention & Erasure

Inquiry records are retained in our secure operational database for up to twelve (12) months following the conclusion of commercial discussions, or until you request erasure, after which records are permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

You may request data access, correction, or permanent erasure at any time by contacting our Data Grievance Officer at privacy@warhawk.aero.

4. Enterprise Drone Aerial Surveillance & Operational Data

Warhawk's commercial drone flight operations are subject to dedicated contractual and regulatory data governance:

  • Authorized Site Boundaries: Flight paths and sensor payloads (RGB, thermal FLIR, LiDAR) are strictly operated within the geographic coordinates and perimeter boundaries authorized by the site owner/operator.
  • Incidental Capture & Privacy Protection: Where ground personnel or members of the public are incidentally captured during perimeter security or thermal inspection flights, automated facial and license-plate blurring can be applied at edge ingestion.
  • Human Supervisory Review: AI computer vision algorithms flag physical observable anomalies (e.g., perimeter breaches, thermal hotspots, loitering vehicles); AI models do not make legal or punitive determinations.
  • Evidence & Client Segregation: Recorded video feeds and inspection reports are cryptographically isolated per enterprise client with strict role-based access controls.

5. Security Controls & Data Storage

All data is stored in enterprise-grade, encrypted environments utilizing industry-standard TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. Access to backend operational systems requires multi-factor authentication, cryptographic session controls, and strict least-privilege role boundaries.

6. Data Grievance Officer

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, any privacy inquiries, grievance redressals, or deletion requests may be directed to:

Warhawk Aerospace Data Grievance Cell
Email: privacy@warhawk.aero
